Ice Breaker spotlight

Score: each player's first-goal chance in his game, weighted by how much scoring his game is expected to have versus tonight's slate average — tonight's top pick is 100.

Method: each skater's share of their game's scoring rate, with a +25% opening-line premium for estimated L1 skaters and a small home-ice tilt, renormalized per game. The starter premium and home tilt are research-backed baselines — not validated absolute rates. Lines are estimated from ice time, never official..
