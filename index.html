About Bueno Sports

Bueno Sports is a model shop, not a tip sheet. Every price on this site comes out of a statistical model we built and grade ourselves — anytime goals in the NHL, home runs in MLB, player props in the NBA — priced as fair, no-vig odds from live data.

The models run on free, public data (the NHL's own API, ESPN, the MLB Stats API) and weigh what actually moves a prop: form, ice time and line deployment, the matchup, the goalie or pitcher, and where a player does his damage on the ice. Two engines check each other — an exact analytical model and a correlated Monte Carlo simulation — so a number has to survive both before it makes the board.

The house rule is simple: backtest first, keep what wins. Every model is graded against real results, every night. Ideas that don't beat the version already live get dropped, however good they sound — and when the model is wrong, the miss stays on the record.

Bueno Sports is a probabilities project, not betting advice. A fair price tells you what a prop is worth, not what will happen.
